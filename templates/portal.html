Your keys

Security — your signing key

This key signs everything issued for your organisation (credentials, export bundles) so anyone can check it's genuine. Rotate it if you believe it may have been exposed — anything already issued stays valid; only new items use the new key.

Template protection (revocable biometrics)

Your people's stored face/print templates are kept in a scrambled, revocable form — a stolen copy can't be matched anywhere else, and can be cancelled like a password. If you ever suspect a leak, reissue: old copies stop working instantly and nobody has to re-enrol.

ID credentials (offline QR cards)

Issue your people signed QR credentials — printed or on their phone, anyone you authorise verifies them in seconds, fully offline, without touching your database. Revocable and expiring.

Access policies (who is allowed, when)

Verification says who; a policy says whether they're allowed right now (schedules, groups, bans). Advise only reports; Enforce denies with access_denied. Off by default — nothing changes until you switch it on.

Guest passes (identities that expire)

Time-box a person: after expiry they stop verifying (identity_expired) until you extend the pass. QR cards issued to guests never outlive the pass.

Devices (your kiosk fleet)

Give every kiosk its own identity: mint a pairing code (15 min, single use), enter it on the device, and it gets its own verify key. Disabling cuts one device off instantly.

Guardians (verify on someone's behalf)

For people who can't present a biometric (children, elderly, patients): link a guardian — their own live verification then counts for the beneficiary. Your integration passes on_behalf_of to /v1/verify.

Consent (lawful basis & withdrawal)

Every enrolment records the person's consent against your statement below (versioned — history stays pinned to the text they saw). A withdrawal blocks their verification immediately and revokes their QR cards.

Trusted organisations (accept their cards)

Add another organisation to accept the credentials they issue — your verifiers then check their cards too. No data import, no integration: trust the issuer, scan their cards.
