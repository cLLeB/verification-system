How your biometrics are protected

Your face or hand photo is never stored. It is turned into a short list of numbers (a template), scrambled with a secret code, and encrypted. The scrambled version can't be turned back into your face, and it only works here — a stolen copy is useless anywhere else.

If a leak is ever suspected, the organisation presses one button to re-scramble everything: old copies stop working instantly, like a password reset, and you don't have to enrol again.
